# DR Davis 10-Day Program - Complete Guide

## Overview
The DR Davis 10-day program is the foundation of the Infinite Health lifestyle, focusing on detoxification/withdrawal from grains and sugars, followed by nutritional supplementation and simple meal planning.

## Key Principles
- **Duration**: 10 days (foundation for 42-day program)
- **Focus**: Detoxification/withdrawal from gliadin-derived opiates and added sugars
- **Goal**: Establish foundation for extraordinary health, weight control, and younger appearance

## Detoxification/Withdrawal Process

### What to Expect
- **Timeline**: Typically 5-7 days, sometimes shorter or longer
- **Symptoms**: Nausea, headache, fatigue, depression
- **Cause**: Removal of gliadin-derived opiates from wheat/grains and added sugars
- **Frequency**: About half of participants experience this process

### Important Notes
- If you don't experience symptoms, you're among the lucky ones
- Symptoms are temporary and will end in several days
- Benefits extend far beyond weight loss (eczema reversal, IBS improvement, fibromyalgia relief, etc.)
- Dramatic reduction in appetite and food cravings

## Managing the Withdrawal Process

### Hydration
- Hydrate more than usual
- Watch urine color (should be light or clear, never dark/amber/brown)
- Don't count fixed ounces - vary based on individual needs
- Lightheadedness often indicates insufficient hydration

### Salt Intake
- Salt food lightly to moderately
- Helps prevent lightheadedness and muscle cramps
- Exceptions: Only if doctor orders severe salt restriction for kidney disease/heart failure

### Exercise
- **Avoid strenuous exercise** during this period
- Light activities are fine: casual walking, leisurely biking
- Don't feel guilty for not exercising - it's counterproductive during detox

### Self-Care
- Pamper yourself: watch funny movies, call friends, get massages
- Recognize symptoms are temporary
- Some conditions may temporarily worsen (migraines, joint pain, rashes) - typically resolves in 3 days

### Muscle Cramps
- If cramps occur (especially in calves/fingers):
  - Hydrate and salt as above
  - Initiate magnesium supplementation
  - Consider magnesium water (recipe in Undoctored book)

## Nutritional Supplements

### Daily Regimen

#### Morning
- **Vitamin D**: 6000 units per day (gelcaps)
- **Fish Oil**: Half daily dose (1800 mg EPA + DHA total)
- **Magnesium**: Magnesium malate (1250 mg tablet = 140 mg elemental)
- **Iodine**: Kelp tablets or drops (400-500 mcg per day)

#### Evening
- **Fish Oil**: Remaining half of daily dose
- **Magnesium**: Repeat morning dose
- **Probiotic**: Daily

### Prebiotic Fiber Schedule

#### Day 3
- **Start**: 10 grams per day (starting dose to minimize GI symptoms)
- **Options**: Inulin powder, jicama, small servings of legumes, chickpeas
- **Timing**: Vary throughout day (morning shake, lunch snack, evening)
- **If symptoms occur**: Stop prebiotics, continue probiotics, retry in 4 weeks

#### Day 10
- **Increase to**: 20 grams per day (full daily dose)
- **If symptoms recur**: Stop prebiotics, continue probiotics, retry in 4 weeks
- **Long-term**: Continue prebiotics for life, probiotics for 6-8 weeks (longer for GI conditions)

## Diet and Meals

### First 10 Days Approach
- **Keep it simple** - no complex recipes needed
- **Use familiar dishes** you already know how to prepare
- **Focus on Infinite Health principles** through simple meals

### Sample Meals

#### Breakfast
- 3 fried eggs
- Sausage
- Coffee with cream

#### Dinner
- Steak or ground beef (no bun)
- Steamed broccoli or asparagus

### After 10 Days
- Introduce new recipes and meal plans
- Recreate unhealthy dishes with healthy ingredients
- More elaborate and interesting food options

## Special Considerations

### Diabetes and Hypertension
- **Critical**: Discuss medication reduction with healthcare provider BEFORE starting
- Blood sugar and pressure can drop precipitously within first 24 hours
- **Dangerous**: Hypoglycemia and hypotension can be life-threatening
- **Medications to discuss**: Insulin, glyburide, glipizide, glimepiride, blood pressure drugs

### Healthcare Provider Issues
- If provider is uncooperative, find functional medicine or integrative health practitioner
- Look for someone who empowers rather than obstructs your health
- Safety is paramount - don't proceed without proper medical guidance

## Program Timeline

### Week 1 (Days 1-7)
- Begin supplements
- Experience detox/withdrawal symptoms
- Simple meals
- Focus on hydration and self-care

### Week 2 (Days 8-10)
- Add prebiotic fibers (Day 10: increase to 20g)
- Symptoms should be improving
- Continue simple meal approach
- Prepare for next phase

## Success Indicators
- Reduced inflammation and water retention
- Improved energy levels
- Better appetite control
- Reduction in chronic symptoms
- Visible health improvements

## Next Steps
After completing the 10-day foundation, the program continues for an additional 32 days (total 42 days) where benefits consolidate and expand further.
