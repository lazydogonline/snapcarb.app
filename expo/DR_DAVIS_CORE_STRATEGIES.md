# DR Davis Infinite Health - Core Strategies

## 🎯 **Core Program Focus**
- **Primary Goal**: Keep blood sugar steady (avoid spikes/rollercoasters)
- **Key Strategy**: Cap net carbs at 15g per meal (or 6-hour digestive window)
- **Net Carbs Formula**: Total Carbs - Fiber = Net Carbs

## 🍽️ **Nutrition Principles**

### **Food Philosophy**
- **Food is Fuel**: Eat only when truly hungry, not out of habit
- **Listen to Your Body**: Don't eat if not hungry, even if it's "breakfast time"
- **Make Meals Count**: Focus on high-quality, nourishing foods

### **Carb Management**
- **15g Net Carbs per meal maximum**
- **6-hour digestive window** between carb resets
- **Track for 7-10 days** to learn your foods, then stop
- **Use FatSecret app** to look up carb counts for whole foods

### **Keep It Simple Strategy**
- **Quick Meals**: Pre-bagged coleslaw mix + protein + nuts
- **Meat & Cheese Rollups**: Ham, cheese, hummus/guacamole, lettuce
- **Breakfast for Any Meal**: Eggs, bacon, omelets
- **High Mileage Foods**: Green beans, almonds, bacon (way under 15g)

### **Food Guidelines**
- **Avoid**: Wheat, grains, seed oils, processed foods, soy
- **Focus On**: Whole foods, healthy fats, quality proteins, vegetables
- **Ready-Made Options**: Base Culture bread, Unbun, Fat Snacks crackers
- **Fermented Foods**: All My Pickle products, homemade yogurt

## 💊 **Core Supplements**

### **Essential Four**
1. **Vitamin D**: 6000 units daily (avoid soybean oil brands)
2. **Fish Oil**: 1800mg EPA + DHA total (split AM/PM)
3. **Magnesium**: Magnesium malate (1250mg = 140mg elemental)
4. **Iodine**: 400-500mcg daily (kelp tablets or drops)

### **Probiotics**
- **Start with**: Probiotic supplements
- **Continue**: 6-8 weeks minimum (longer for GI conditions)
- **Yogurt Projects**: 36-hour fermentation for maximum benefits

## 🌱 **Prebiotic Fibers**

### **Start Slow Strategy**
- **Begin with**: 1/2 teaspoon inulin in coffee/tea
- **Listen to body**: Stop if digestive distress occurs
- **Wait 4 weeks** if symptoms, then retry
- **Get food and supplements working first** before adding prebiotics

### **Prebiotic Sources**
- **Inulin Powder**: Add to coffee, smoothies, water
- **Acacia Powder**: Mix with water before adding supplements
- **Raw White Potato**: Zero net carbs, shred on salads, use as dipper
- **Green Bananas**: Zero net carbs, freeze for smoothies
- **Beans/Lentils**: Count carbs, pair with low-carb foods
- **Fermented Vegetables**: Sauerkraut, kimchi, fermented potatoes

### **Prebiotic Tips**
- **Powder first, then water** (prevents clumping)
- **Gentle stirring only** (don't blend yogurt microbes)
- **Track reactions** to identify what works for your body
- **Ferment potatoes** if you have excess (recipe on Inner Circle)

### **Blood Sugar Monitoring**
- **Target**: Minimal change (under 10 points) after meals
- **Timing**: Test before meal, then 30-60 minutes after
- **Tools**: Finger stick meters, Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGM)
- **Goal**: Keep blood sugar under 100 fasting, avoid spikes

## 🚫 **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

### **Don't Overcomplicate**
- **No calorie counting** - focus only on net carbs
- **No rigid meal timing** - eat when hungry, not by clock
- **No protein percentage limits** - focus on carb management
- **Don't blend yogurt** - gently stir to preserve microbes

### **Don't Rush**
- **Start with food** - get comfortable with carb counting
- **Add supplements gradually** - one at a time
- **Wait for prebiotics** - ensure gut is ready
- **Listen to your body** - it will tell you what it needs

## 🔑 **Success Keys**

### **Mindset Shifts**
- **From "diet" to "lifestyle"** - this is permanent health restoration
- **From "calorie restriction" to "carb management"** - focus on blood sugar
- **From "three meals daily" to "eat when hungry"** - listen to your body
- **From "quick fixes" to "gradual healing"** - patience with the process

### **Daily Practices**
- **Plan meals** like a restaurant menu (options, not rigid schedule)
- **Track carbs** for 7-10 days to learn your foods
- **Take supplements** consistently (use AM/PM containers)
- **Monitor blood sugar** if you want data-driven approach
- **Search Inner Circle** for specific questions and advanced topics
