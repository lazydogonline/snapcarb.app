# DR Davis Infinite Health - Complete Cheat Sheets

## 🚨 **CORE PRINCIPLE: CALORIES DON'T MATTER!**
**Only track NET CARBS: 15g maximum per meal**
- Net Carbs = Total Carbs - Fiber
- No calorie counting needed
- Focus on carb quality, not quantity

---

## 🍽️ **10-DAY DETOX PROGRAM**

### **3 STEPS TO BEGIN**
1. **ELIMINATE** all foods made with wheat and grains (rye, barley, corn, oats, millet, sorghum, rice, amaranth, triticale, bulgur)
2. **EAT** real, single-ingredient foods: eggs, avocados, green peppers, pork chops
3. **MANAGE** carbohydrates—no more than 15 grams net carbs per meal

### **SURVIVING WHEAT/GRAIN WITHDRAWAL**
- Choose a non-stressful period
- Hydrate well—more than usual, add sea salt to food
- Pamper yourself—massage, funny movies, naps
- Don't exercise—casual activity only
- **This is a necessary process for feeling great again!**

### **WHAT TO EXPECT FIRST WEEK**
Many experience opiate-like withdrawal syndrome:
- Fatigue, Low Mood, Nausea, Headache, Dizziness
- **It is a necessary process for recovery!**

### **BE SURE NOT TO**
- Let wheat/grains sneak into your diet
- Count calories—calories do not matter!
- Limit fat
- Have more than one alcoholic beverage per day (if weight loss is a goal)

### **FIGHT HUNGER AND CRAVINGS BY**
- Including more fats and oils
- Eating a Fat Blaster
- Eating a Detox Fat Ball
- **If you are hungry, you are not eating enough fat and food!**

## 💊 **NUTRITIONAL SUPPLEMENTS**

### **Essential Four**
1. **Vitamin D**: 4000-6000 units per day as gelcaps (adjust to blood level 60-70 ng/mL)
2. **Fish Oil**: 3000-3600 mg total per day (EPA+DHA, not capsule weight) divided in two doses
3. **Magnesium Malate**: 1200 mg twice per day (or 180 mg twice per day of elemental magnesium)
4. **Iodine**: 500 mcg per day, drops or kelp tablets (unless you have thyroiditis)
5. **High-potency Probiotic**: 50 billion CFU per day or more with at least a dozen bacterial species

## 🍯 **APPROVED SWEETENERS**

### **Safe Options**
1. **Stevia**: Pure liquid, pure powdered, or powdered with inulin (avoid maltodextrin)
2. **Monk Fruit**: Also known as lo han guo, can be tricky to find
3. **Erythritol**: Natural sweetener found in fruit, ~70% sweetness of sugar
   - ⚠️ **LIMIT/ELIMINATE** due to 2023 cardiovascular risk study
4. **Xylitol**: Most sugar-like, can be used for glazing
   - ⚠️ **TOXIC TO DOGS!**
5. **Inulin**: Fiber sweetener, best used in combination with other sweeteners
6. **Allulose**: Newest prebiotic sweetener, browns readily
   - ⚠️ Watch for maltodextrin or other unsafe additives

## 📱 **FOR YOUR APP IMPLEMENTATION**

### **Key Principles to Emphasize**
1. **NO CALORIE COUNTING** - Remove all calorie displays
2. **ONLY NET CARBS MATTER** - 15g maximum per meal
3. **FAT IS YOUR FRIEND** - Encourage healthy fat consumption
4. **REAL FOODS ONLY** - Single-ingredient, unprocessed foods

### **When Suggesting Food Swaps**
- Always check net carb content
- Recommend from approved lists above
- Emphasize fat content over protein
- Suggest approved flours for baking

### **Health Tracking Focus**
- Blood glucose (FBG & PPBG)
- Weight and waist measurements
- Blood pressure
- Supplement tracking
- **NOT calorie tracking**
