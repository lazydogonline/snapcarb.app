# DR Davis Program Markers - Complete Reference

## 🎯 **Essential Health Markers & Target Values**

### **Blood Glucose & Insulin (Most Critical)**
- **HbA1c**: 4.0% to 5.0% (NGSP) / 20-31 mmol/mol (IFCC)
- **Fasting Blood Glucose (FBG)**: 70-90 mg/dL (3.9-5.0 mmol/L)
- **Postprandial Blood Glucose (PPBG)**: ≤100 mg/dL (5.5 mmol/L), ideally no rise from FBG
- **Fasting Insulin**: 0-4 mIU/L (0-27.8 pmol/L)

### **Body Composition**
- **BMI**: 18.5-24.5 kg/m²
- **Weight & Waist**: Track for visceral fat reduction progress

### **Cardiovascular Markers**
- **Triglycerides (TG)**: 30-60 mg/dL (0.34-0.68 mmol/L)
- **HDL**: 60-240 mg/dL (1.55-6.2 mmol/L)
- **NMR Small LDL-P**: 0-200 nmol/L
- **Lipoprotein(a)**: 0-50 nmol/L

### **Blood Pressure**
- **Systolic**: 90-115 mmHg (12-15.3 kPa)
- **Diastolic**: 50-70 mmHg (6.7-9.3 kPa)

### **Calcification**
- **Coronary Calcium Score (CCS)**: Zero (0) - if non-zero, growth -100% to +10% per year

### **Thyroid Function**
- **TSH**: 0.2-2.0 µIU/mL (mIU/L)
- **fT3**: Upper half of lab reference range
- **fT4**: Upper half of lab reference range
- **rT3**: Lower half of reference range
- **Temperature (arising oral)**: 97.3°F (36.3°C) or higher
- **Thyroid Antibodies**: Within normal lab range

### **Micronutrients**
- **RBC Magnesium**: Upper half of lab reference range
- **25-OH Vitamin D₃**: 60-70 ng/mL (150-180 nmol/L)
- **Omega-3 Index**: 10.0-12.0%
- **Omega-6:3 Ratio**: 0-2:1
- **Vitamin B₁₂**: Upper half of reference range
- **MMA**: Upper half of reference range
- **Iron & Ferritin**: Conventional targets apply
- **Zinc**: Conventional targets apply

### **Inflammation & Blood Health**
- **CRP/hsCRP**: 0-1.0 mg/L (zero is fine)
- **Fibrinogen**: 200-350 mg/mL
- **Homocysteine**: 0-10 µmol/L

### **Heavy Metals**
- **Cadmium (Cd)**: Not to exceed U.S. Dept. of Labor standards
- **Mercury (Hg)**: ≤10 μg/L

### **Gut Health**
- **Prebiotic Fiber Challenge**: 20 grams/day, mixed & varied
- **H₂ Breath Test**: <20 ppm above baseline
- **CH₄ Breath Test**: Pending (research in progress)
- **H₂S Breath Test**: Pending (research in progress)

### **Genetic Markers**
- **Apo E**: One-time test (E3:3 requires no extra attention)

## 🧪 **Testing Strategy & Tips**

### **Getting Tests Done**
1. **Insurance Coverage**: Check annual check-up coverage first
2. **Doctor Orders**: Request tests through your healthcare provider
3. **Independent Testing**: Use services like Direct Labs, Quest Diagnostics, Life Extension
4. **CPT Codes**: Use specific codes for precise test requests

### **Test Timing**
- **Lipid Panels**: Only when weight stable for 30+ days
- **HbA1c**: Every 90 days or longer
- **Home Blood Glucose**: Daily for FBG, 30-60 min post-meal for PPBG
- **Independent Tests**: Do early to bring results to doctor visits

### **Why 90 Days? The Cell Renewal Cycle** 🧬
- **Red blood cells live ~120 days** - your body constantly replaces them
- **Glycated (damaged) cells from poor glucose control** get renewed every 90-120 days
- **HbA1c at 90 days** = Perfect timing to see if your **damaged cells are being replaced with healthy ones**
- **This is when you see the TRUE healing** - not just symptom improvement, but **cellular regeneration**

### **Home Testing Options**
- **Blood Glucose Meters**: ~$20 for FBG and PPBG monitoring
- **CGM Devices**: OTC versions now available (calibrate and aim for 68-90 range)
- **Breath Testing**: AIRE devices for H₂ testing (~$200, reusable)
- **Home Kits**: Some tests available as mail-away options
